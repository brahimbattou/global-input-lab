# Support & Community Resources

Thank you for using **GlobalInputLab**!

Whether you are debugging a complex Unicode encoding corruption in your database, implementing international input fuzzing in your CI pipeline, or encountering unexpected behavior with a particular script, here are the best channels to receive help.

---

## 1. Documentation & References

Before opening an issue, check our reference guides:
- [README.md](README.md): Quick start, CLI commands, and programmatic API examples.
- [Architecture Guide](docs/architecture.md): Deep-dive into graphemes, Unicode standard annexes, and test runner internals.
- [Product Research](RESEARCH.md): Competitor comparison, problem analysis, and standard references.

---

## 2. GitHub Discussions & Q&A

For general questions, architectural guidance, or sharing test vectors:
- **Discussions:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/brahimbattou/global-input-lab/discussions)
  - **Q&A:** Ask how to test specific APIs or frameworks.
  - **Show and Tell:** Share how your team integrates GlobalInputLab in CI.
  - **Ideas:** Propose new test vector categories or features.

---

## 3. Bug Reports & Feature Requests

If you found a bug or have a feature suggestion:
- **Bug Reports:** Open a [Bug Report](https://github.com/brahimbattou/global-input-lab/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with your minimal reproduction payload and environment details.
- **New Vectors:** Open a [New Test Vector Request](https://github.com/brahimbattou/global-input-lab/issues/new?template=new_vector.yml).
- **Feature Requests:** Open a [Feature Request](https://github.com/brahimbattou/global-input-lab/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

---

## 4. Security Vulnerabilities

To report security issues, please follow our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and email `security@battou.dev`. Do not open public issues for security vulnerabilities.
